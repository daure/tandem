## Scratch workspace

Use this folder for notes, research, and small local experiments. Keep work inside the workspace.
The template seeds `notes.md`, `research/sources.md`, and `experiments/README.md` into the workspace.
Record the objective, findings, and next steps in `notes.md`; distinguish verified facts from assumptions.
Cite evidence in `research/sources.md` and keep experiments under `experiments/`.
Read existing notes before changing them and preserve user edits.

Before handing over, check that referenced local files exist and that any commands you documented
have been verified. Ask before installing dependencies, initializing Git, or using external services.
